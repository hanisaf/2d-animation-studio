# Scene 17: The Broccoli Directive

> Halloween night. The robot the girls dreamed up in scene 16 wakes up with one directive. Robo-Safadi chases Housam, Alma, Jenna and Danny one at a time, takes their candy and makes them eat broccoli. Then he catches the Safadi twins in the confiscated-candy bowl. *"Everybody eats broccoli."*

**Length:** 116 s, cut to the song `audio.mp3` (*The Broccoli Directive*, 126 BPM).
**Style:** a music video like `../PDoomVideo`. There is no set: every shot is a motion-graphics backdrop in Halloween colours (night violet, pumpkin orange, slime green, candy pink), and the camera always pushes, shakes or bounces on the beat. Robo's lyrics run as karaoke along the bottom, and each word lights up as he sings it.
