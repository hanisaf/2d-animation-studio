## The story

This is the finale of a kids' cartoon series. Two grown-up twin brothers, **Professor Safadi** (a professor in a tan suit) and **Doctor Sami** (a doctor in a white coat), have been sneaking video-game time while telling the kids to limit their screen time. After a family board-game night they fall asleep on the sofa, and they **dream they are inside their video games** together with the four kids: **Housam** (a teenage soccer fan), **Alma** (a little girl who dances), **Jenna** (her cousin, a gymnast) and **Danny** (a funny six-year-old who loves building blocks). Other characters from the show appear as guests: King Neptune (a plush dolphin with a trident), Robo-Safadi (a robot copy of the professor who wants everyone to eat broccoli), Clawd (a little orange AI hologram), Jester Fester (an unlucky jester whose ball always ends up hitting his head) and Princess Pearl (a dolphin princess).

They play through four games and win every level as **one team**, then face the final boss: **the alarm clock**. They wake up in the morning to the kids holding a ringing clock and the board games, and everyone laughs. The message is warm and funny: playing **together** beats playing alone.

  - the two brothers: warm, older and a bit goofy;
