## Synopsis

**The planning.** A crisp October afternoon in front of the house. Alma and Jenna are on the lawn with Jenna's sketchbook: Halloween is one week away and they want *the spookiest house on the street*. Every idea pops out of Jenna's pencil as a doodle drawn over the real house: **ghosts** in the bushes, giant **spiders** on the porch, **black cats** on the step, **pumpkins** down the walk, and a **witch** who crashed into the chimney. Then Jenna frowns: *everybody* has those. Alma's eyes turn to stars: a **vampire dolphin**! Jenna holds up her giraffe plush: a **mummy giraffe**, so tall it peeks over the roof! *"SPOOKY-CUTE!"*

**The professor.** Professor Safadi strolls up the garden walk. *"What's going on here?"* The girls tell him: Halloween decorations. *"And why are you so interested in Halloween?"* Together: **"CANDY!"** Safadi reminds Alma of her promise (scene 04). She has an answer ready: the promise was for *every day*, and Halloween is a *holiday*. Safadi smiles a slow smile: *"May I… explain something?"*

**The lecture.** His powers switch on: the glow, the orbiting formulas, the floating board. Food is fuel and veggies are *super fuel*; candy is a quick zoom and a big crash; sugar feeds the tiny sugar bugs on your teeth. The girls wilt. Safadi turns to his board…

**The whisper.** …and behind his back, Alma leans to Jenna: *"Psst… I've got the spookiest idea."* *"Are you thinking what I'm thinking?"* Jenna turns her sketchbook so only Alma (and we) can see it: **ROBO-SAFADI**, a boxy robot professor in a tan suit with a bolt on each side of his big head. Its **red eyes activate**, *BZZT*: *"EAT. YOUR. BROCCOLI."* The girls clap their hands over their mouths, giggling.

**The cliffhanger.** Safadi stops mid-sentence and turns around. *"…What's so funny?"* Two sweet, innocent smiles: *"Oh, nothing…"* and a wink: **"You shall see!"** Safadi looks at us, one brow up. In the sketchbook, two red eyes glint. **TO BE CONTINUED…**

**Theme:** a lecture never beats a good idea, and the best Halloween decorations are the ones that *mean* something to you, even if that something is a professor.

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`)

- `DRAW`: the windows when Jenna's pencil scribbles. `TRAD` and `FAVS`: each doodle's kind, world point, height and start time. The traditional ones POOF out at 25.0; the favourites fade after SPOOKY-CUTE.
- `WALK` is Safadi's entrance (garden walk, then across the lawn); `POWER` the lecture (aura, glowing eyes, boards). `BOARD` is the board's world point, so every shot agrees where it floats; it's drawn only when it fits in frame.
- `almaPose`, `jennaPose` and `safadiPose` hold each character's acting over the whole scene, so a pose carries across cuts. Shots only choose the camera.
- Jenna holds the sketchbook (cover to us) and pencil throughout, except for the giraffe beat (30.4–33.8), when the book lies on the grass.
- The set is the House with `season: 'autumn'`.
