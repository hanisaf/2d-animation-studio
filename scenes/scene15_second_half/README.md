# Scene 15: The Second Half

> Half-time. The twins chuckle, sure of the win. The kids huddle, and Housam cracks the trap: *you can't be offside if you're dribbling the ball yourself.* Danny dribbles past Safadi and scores while Sami snoozes (*"OFFSIDE!"* *"NOT!"*). Alma saves, then dribbles the length of the pitch, survives an ice-cream daydream and scores. The twins fight back: two penalties and a wonder strike from Sami's own goal make it 3–2. Housam levels with a top-corner shot, and Sami's free kick goes straight to Housam, who wins it 4–3. *"You earned it… go grab an energy drink from the cooler."* Alma knows that trick. The kids head to the water fountain; the twins open the cooler, and this time the bottles say *100% electrolytes · 100% sugar · 100% fun*. **The End.**

## Why the plan works (so the calls are right)

**You can't be offside if nobody passes to you.** Offside is only judged when a teammate *plays* the ball forward to you. A player who dribbles the ball past the last defender and shoots is never offside, however few opponents are left. So the kids stop passing forward in the Adults' half: whoever has the ball takes it past Safadi alone and shoots. Sami's *"OFFSIDE!"* after Danny's goal is simply wrong, which is why everyone shouts *"NOT!"*.

The other calls:
- **Penalties:** Jenna and Alma each trip Safadi inside their own penalty area, so both are penalties. Alma's trip is reckless, hence the **yellow card**.
- **Sami's goal:** a goalkeeper may shoot from his own area; it bounces the whole length of the pitch over Alma, who is off her line.
- **The winner:** Sami's free kick is an accident: he passes it straight to Housam.

## Synopsis

**Half-time.** The twins sit on the Adults' bench, feet up, sharing a thermos. *"Zero–zero, brother. They can't score if they're always offside."* *"Heh heh heh."*

**The huddle.** The kids huddle on the grass by their bench, glum. Jenna draws the problem in the grass with a stick (her comics skills): a goal, Sami, and Safadi far away. *"Every time we pass… offside!"* Housam stares at the drawing, eyes narrowing; the angles and lines appear around him the way they do when he thinks (scene 09's math vision). *"Wait. Offside is only when we PASS forward."* *"So… we don't pass?"* *"We DRIBBLE. Take it past Professor Safadi yourself, then shoot!"* Four hands stack: *"Team Kids!"*

**Goal 1: Danny.** Fester whistles for the second half. Safadi dribbles forward, humming, but Danny slide-tackles him clean and pops up with the ball. Danny winds up to pass to Housam, who waves his arms: *"Danny! Remember the plan!"* Danny grins, tucks the ball close, and zooms past a flailing Safadi. Sami is asleep on the post again. Safadi, chasing: *"Wake up, BRO!"* Too late. Danny scores. Sami jolts awake and points confidently: *"OFFSIDE!"* The kids and Fester, together: **"NOT!"** Fester: *"He dribbled it himself. Goal!"* **1–0.**

**Goal 2: Alma.** Safadi charges down the other end and shoots; Alma saves it. Safadi plants himself next to her goal, waiting for her pass. Alma pulls her arm back to throw it to Jenna, stops, and taps her head: the plan. She drops the ball and dribbles out of her goal. Safadi lumbers after her: *"Come back here!"* Too slow. Alma runs the whole pitch, music notes trailing her feet. Sami, awake this time, steps out to meet her with his kindest doctor smile: *"Do you want an… ice cream?"* Alma's eyes turn to hearts; a dream bubble opens: a giant rainbow ice-cream cone with sprinkles. While she drifts, Sami charges at her. Housam, from behind: *"Sister, WAKE UP!"* The bubble pops. Alma blinks, sidesteps Sami at the last moment, and shoots. **"GOAAAL!"** **2–0.**

**The comeback.** The twins slump, panting, sweat flying. *"They're… good."* *"Too good."* Then Safadi snatches the ball and storms up the pitch into the Kids' box, where Jenna sticks out a foot and trips him: FWEET! Fester points to the spot: *"Penalty!"* Safadi takes it, a slow, smug run-up, and scores. **2–1.** From the restart, the ball pops back to Sami, who boots it from his own goal; it bounces once, twice, three times down the whole pitch, over Alma's head, and in. Sami, astonished: *"Did I do that?"* **2–2.** Then Safadi steals the ball from Danny, dribbles into the box, and Alma dives at his feet and trips him. FWEET! Fester holds up a **yellow card** to Alma: *"Penalty!"* Safadi scores again. **2–3**; the twins chest-bump and dance.

**The finish.** Housam takes the kick-off, dribbles past Safadi to the top of the box, and curls it into the top-left corner (his math vision again, the angle glowing). **3–3.** Fester awards the Adults a free kick; Sami takes it and accidentally passes it straight to Housam. *"Oops."* Housam charges up the pitch alone and scores. **4–3.** Fester blows the final whistle: *"FULL TIME! The Kids win!"* The kids pile up; Jenna cartwheels; Danny does his goofy dance.

**The cooler.** Safadi strolls over, all smiles. *"Well played, team. You earned it… go grab an energy drink from the cooler."* The kids beam and start toward it, but Alma throws out her arms: *"Stop! He swapped them with unsweetened tea! Don't fall for this trap!"* (a callback to scene 11). The kids nod knowingly and march off to the water fountain by the snack bar, drinking happily. The twins shrug and open the cooler. Sami eyes his bottle: *"Unsweet tea?"* Safadi turns his bottle around: the label reads **100% ELECTROLYTES · 100% SUGAR · 100% FUN**. They clink bottles and drink, chuckling: *"Heh heh heh…"* Iris out. **THE END.**

## Notes for editing

- **Key times** are constants at the top of `scene.js`; goals are in `GOALS` (`scoreAt(t)` drives the scoreboard everywhere), whistles in `WHISTLES`, kicks / celebrations / falls per character in `ACT`.
- **Blocking and the ball** work as in scene 14: `PATHS` (`[t, x, z]` keys, a repeated time is a cut) and `BALL` segments (rest, at someone's feet, a pass or shot with an arc, held).
- **Draw order:** `group()` splits the set just in front of the nearest character (`autoSplit`), so shelters and goals land in front of or behind the cast correctly in both camera directions.
- **Props in this scene:** the cooler and bottles (from scene 11; the bottle has a new `fun` side), the water fountain, Jenna's grass drawing, Housam's math vision, Alma's ice-cream dream bubble, Fester's yellow card, the thermos, sweat drops, THE END card.
- **Set:** `crowd` .5; the fans cheer on every goal (louder for the Kids) and at full time; the scoreboard clock reads HALF TIME at the start and FULL TIME after the final whistle.
