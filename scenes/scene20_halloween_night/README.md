# Scene 20: The Infinite Candy Glitch

> Halloween night at Alma's house. Robo-Safadi stands in the yard; the grown-ups (Professor Safadi, Doctor Sami, Jester Fester and Dragon) can't work out what he does, and the kids only drop hints. After BOO, Spooky and Pumpkin get nothing, Safadi tries **"Trick!"** (broccoli) and Fester **"Treat!"** (candy); the twins work out the conditional. Then: TREAT! TREAT! TREAT!, an **infinite candy glitch**. Safadi frowns: *"Let me **explain**…"* and at that word, Clawd wakes up inside the robot. Red eyes. **"BEEP. BOOP."** Everyone stares. *To be continued…*

## Synopsis

**Halloween night.** From the street, under a full moon and a few bats, up to Alma's house, decorated exactly as the girls planned in scene 16: sheet ghosts in the bushes, a giant web and spider on the porch, black cats on the steps, jack-o'-lanterns down the walk, a witch crashed into the chimney, the vampire dolphin and the mummy giraffe, tombstones on the lawn, and a string of orange, purple and green lights along the porch. The windows glow. Robo-Safadi stands in the middle of the yard, calm cyan eyes. The trick-or-treaters arrive with pumpkin pails: *"Happy Halloween, everybody!"*

**Who is that?** Safadi stops dead: *"Wait… is that… me?!"* Sami: *"Brother… it has your hair!"* Fester: *"And your tie! But what does it do?"* Dragon: *"Does it breathe fire?"* The kids smirk. Alma: *"You'll have to find out yourselves!"* Jenna: *"Hint: it loves Halloween words!"* Danny: *"Magic Halloween words!"* Housam: *"But we're not telling which ones!"* (a finger to the lips, a wink).

**Nothing works.** Fester jumps at it: *"BOO!"* (the robot blinks: "…"). Dragon: *"Spooky…?"* ("…"). Sami fires off *"Pumpkin! Ghost! Candy corn?"* ("…" "…" "…"). Safadi strokes his chin: *"Hmm… Halloween words… Of course! Trick!"* ZIP: a telescoping arm shoots out and hands him a **broccoli**. He's delighted: *"An excellent choice!"* Fester: *"My turn! Treat!"* DING: **candy** in his pail. *"CANDY!!"* Sami: *"Trick!"* Broccoli. *"…Hmm."* Dragon: *"Treat!"* The arm pops a candy straight into his mouth. CHOMP.

**They crack it.** Safadi counts it off: *"Trick… broccoli. Treat… candy…"* The twins, together: *"If trick, then broccoli…"* *"…else if treat, candy! A conditional!"* Housam whispers to Danny: *"They figured it out!"* *"Took them long enough!"*

**The infinite candy glitch.** Fester has an idea: *"Wait… so if I say treat again… TREAT! TREAT! TREAT!"* Candy, candy, candy. Then everyone: **"TREAT! TREAT! TREAT!"** The robot throws both arms up and becomes a candy fountain, flinging candy in arcs into every pail and into Dragon's mouth. Danny: *"It's an infinite candy glitch!"* Pails overflow; candy piles up on the lawn; Fester disappears up to his waist. *"CANDY! CANDY! CANDY!"*

**Let me explain.** Safadi's smile fades; a sweat drop. The fountain stops (record scratch). *"Hmm… wait a moment, children."* His finger goes up, his eyes glow: *"Let me **explain** that this amount of candy is not hea—"*

**BEEP BOOP.** On the word *explain*, close on the robot: click, a hatch in his chest swings open. Inside, curled up, is **Clawd**. His eyes flick open… then turn **red**. Robo-Safadi's eyes blaze red, his antenna sparks, he jolts upright: **"BEEP. BOOP."** Wide: everyone turns to stare at him, "?" over every head. Freeze. **TO BE CONTINUED…**

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`)

- **Blocking** is in `CAST[name].at` and `ROBOT`. Each cast entry also says which hand holds the pumpkin pail (`bucket`; the grown-ups use the right hand, the kids the left, so the pails face the robot) and the kids' costume hat (`hat`, scene 17's `bdHat`). Safadi has no pail; he holds his broccoli.
- **Deliveries:** `GIVE` lists every trick or treat answered by the robot's telescoping arm (`bdArm`): `[time, who, 'broccoli' | 'candy']`. Nonsense words are in `NOTHING` (the robot blinks "…").
- **The glitch:** `FOUNTAIN` is the window in which the robot flings a candy every `GAP` seconds to a pseudo-random recipient (`throws`), each landing after `FLIGHT`. `count(who, t)` is how much candy someone has, which drives their pail's fill and, past 5, the pile on the lawn (`hnPile`; Fester's grows faster and taller, until he's buried to the waist).
- **The ending:** `UNEASY` (the sweat drop and the stop), `EXPLAIN` (the cut on the word), `HATCH` / `WAKE` / `RED` (the chest hatch, Clawd's eyes, the robot's red eyes), `BEEP`, `STARE`, `FREEZE`.
- **Night light:** the set is lit by the House's `night: 1`. The decorations (`decor.js`) get a lighter night tint so they read, then the jack-o'-lanterns and string lights glow on top (`hnGlow`). Each character is drawn through a `brightness(.86)` layer to sit in the night.
- **Dependencies:** this scene uses scene 16's `spkDoodle` (the girls' decorations) and scene 17's `bd*` props (pumpkins, tombstones, bats, pails, candy, broccoli, the arm, the hats). They load before it in registry order.
