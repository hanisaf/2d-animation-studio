# "You Shall See": the song script for Gemini

Paste **Part A** into Gemini as the style prompt, then **Part B** as the lyrics. Words in `[square brackets]` are section and performance tags; words in `(parentheses)` are backing vocals or shouts. Don't paste the timing column from the README; it's only for animating.

---

## Part A: style prompt

> A 3-minute, high-energy kids' pop / funk musical number in the style of a Broadway showstopper crossed with a modern Bruno-Mars-style funk-pop single. 120 BPM, key of C major, with a key change up to D major for the final chorus. Instruments: slap bass, clean funky guitar, a brass section (trumpet stabs and trombone slides), a glockenspiel and music box for the "storybook" moments, handclaps, and a big drum kit. Cartoon sound effects are part of the arrangement: a page-flip whoosh, a cartoon "BONK" on the beat drop, a record scratch.
>
> It is sung by a cast of cartoon characters, each with a distinct voice:
> - **Professor Safadi**: a warm, formal, slightly pompous baritone crooner, like a proud lounge singer
> - **Princess Pearl**: a deadpan, bored, regal diva; mostly spoken, very dry
> - **The kids** (Housam, Alma, Jenna, Danny): a bright, cheeky children's choir; they whisper, then shout
> - **Housam**: a quick, rhythmic, confident boy rapper
> - **Doctor Sami**: a goofy, bouncy, playful tenor
> - **Dragon**: a gentle, low, sweet and excited voice
> - **Robo-Safadi**: a flat, buzzy robot vocoder monotone in clipped syllables
> - **Clawd**: high, fast robot chirps (no words)
> - **Jester Fester**: a soft, hopeful, slightly sad voice that turns triumphant
>
> Structure: an intro fanfare, a swaggering verse, a whispered pre-chorus, a huge sing-along chorus, a comedic rapid-fire verse with a bored diva interrupting, a quiet piano bridge with a tender solo and a fast nerdy rap, a build that cuts to one beat of total silence, then a massive beat drop on a cartoon "BONK", a party section, a key-changed final chorus with a robot counter-melody, and a quiet music-box outro with a comic punchline. The chorus hook "You shall see!" must be extremely catchy and chant-able.

## Tips for generating it

- **Too short?** If Gemini stops before the outro, generate the song in two halves with the same style prompt: everything up to the second chorus, then from `[Bridge]` to the end, and join them on the bridge's piano entry.
- **Voices blending together?** Add the voice description to each tag, e.g. `[Robo-Safadi, robot vocoder monotone]`, and regenerate. The robot, Pearl's dry speaking voice and the kids' choir are the three that matter most for the jokes.
- **The silence before BONK is the whole joke.** If the take has no gap there, regenerate until it does: that beat is the video's thumbnail moment.
- When you have a take you like, save it as `scenes/scene22_you_shall_see/audio.mp3` and the scene can be cut to it (beat tracking and word timings come from the audio, like scene 17).
