# Game Over!

> Straight after [33 Busted!](../scene33_busted/README.md): the four kids huddle in Housam's bedroom. *"So what is the plan? I hope you don't involve Robo-Safadi."* *"My plan is software only this time. Clawd is going to visit the brothers' games!"* *"How can he do that?"* *"Clawd can hop into any game!"* Clawd pops out of Housam's laptop and does a loop-the-loop. *"Now let us teach them a lesson about belittling us children!"* *"Yeah!"* *"But how?"* *"We are going to make their games harder!"* Clawd salutes and zooms out of the window. In the living room he sparkles into the twins' laptops, unnoticed. The twins brag, *"Robo Invasion is the perfect game!"*, *"Sports games remind me of the importance of exercise!"*, and laugh. Inside each game a tiny Clawd turns the DIFFICULTY dial to SUPER HARD. The robots swarm the professor: GAME OVER. *"Impossible!"* *"Let me show you my skills."* The little kids run rings round the doctor: KIDS WIN. *"Impossible!"* Another round, another loss. *"Losing is not fun. Let us take a break."* Clawd zips home. Knock knock: the kids drop in. *"So, how did the research go?"* *"…space…"* *"…importance of sport!"* *"How about we all play board games?"* *"HOORAY!"* *To be continued…*

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`, `props.js`)

- **Timing constants** at the top of `scene.js`: `TO_DESK`, `POP`, `SALUTE`, `ZOOM`, `RIPPLE`, `SPARKLE`, `LAUGH`, `WINK`, `ROBO0`, `SOCCER0`, `REMATCH`, `SHUT`, `KNOCK`, `WALK_IN`, `HOORAY`, `FREEZE`, `END`. Dialogue times live in `asset.js`; move a line there and its beat here together.
- **Blocking (Housam's bedroom, bed view):** Alma (−150, 470), Jenna (−55, 505), Danny (45, 480), Housam (150, 445) on the rug; Housam slides to the desk spot (205, 410) at `TO_DESK`. The laptop is on the desk at (287, 72, 410); Clawd's hologram rises out of it and leaves by the window on the left wall (−340, 170, 390).
- **Blocking (living room, sofa view, so +X is screen-left):** the twins sit as in scene 33 (Safadi X 95, Sami X −95 on the sofa, Z 85, drawn low and clipped at the seat). Clawd's sparkle comes in at the window (−440, 170, 560). The kids come in from the archway (420, 270) and stand two each side of the sofa: Housam (350, 300), Alma (265, 250) on screen-left, Jenna (−265, 250), Danny (−350, 300) on screen-right.
- **The screens** (`props.js`): `goDial` is tiny Clawd turning the DIFFICULTY dial in a game's corner; `goRoboHard` and `goSoccerHard` are the super-hard games, inside `goLaptop`'s laptop frame with the player's shoulder. They reuse scene 32's `stArcade`, `stFaceGlow` and `ST_WC`, so scene 32 must stay registered before this one.
- **The twins' laptops:** `goLid` draws each lid toward us; `red` (0..1) turns its light red, `peek` (0..1) pops a tiny winking Clawd out of it, and `shut` (0..1) folds it down.
