# Screen Time

> After the clean-up the kids promised to play board games, but when Professor Safadi drops in, all four of them are glued to their tablets. *"Children, you said you wanted to play board games!"* It takes a long time to get an answer. They're done with board games, and they're still playing *together*: Alma plays *Craft Blocks* with Jenna, Housam plays *Fight Planet* with Danny. *"Do you remember what I said about video games?"* *"No."* Dr. Sami drops in: *"30 minutes per day, and I suppose it is over!"* The kids hit back: *"But you are also on your screen more than 30 minutes a day!"* Cut to the study, where the professor is "researching his books" with *Robo Invasion*, and to the clinic, where the doctor is "looking up medical information" with *World Champion*. *"This is not fair!"* *"Your screen time is up. Go play outside!"* The kids trudge out. *"Brother, what shall we do now?"* *"Robo Invasion… or World Champion?"* They laugh. *To be continued…*

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`)

- **Timing constants** at the top: `KNOCK`, `SAF_IN`, `SEES`, `AHEM`, `WAVE`, `SAMI_IN`, `LOOK_UP`, `GULP`, the ripples `RIP_S`, `RIP_R1`, `RIP_C`, `RIP_R2`, the pushes `ZOOM_S` / `ZOOM_C`, the games `ROBO` / `SOCCER`, `DOWN` (tablets on the rug), `OUT` (each kid's walk to the door), `LAUGH`, `FREEZE`, `END`. Dialogue times live in `asset.js`; move a line there and its beat in the pose functions together.
- **Blocking (bed view):** Jenna (−230, 400), Danny (−135, 370), Housam (−40, 395), Alma (60, 450); the board-game stack on the rug at (−95, 470); Professor Safadi (240, 330); Dr. Sami (150, 245). `at(name, t)` walks the twins in from the doorway (220, 50) and the kids out through it.
- **The tablets** (`props.js`, prefix `st`): `stTablet` draws one held at the chest (we see its back and the blue glow on the face), `stTabletFloor` one lying on the rug, and `stScreen(kind, …)` paints a tablet's screen (`'craft'` or `'fight'`) for the inserts. `GAMES` in `scene.js` says who plays what.
- **The video games** are full-frame shots in a monitor bezel (`stMonitor`): `roboGame` draws a grid of real `roboSafadi` rigs (no boil, small scale) that march on the beat, and `neptune` at the bottom firing `neptuneBlast`; `soccerGame` is a side-on pitch with real `sami`, `housam`, `danny`, `jenna` and `alma` rigs. Each invader pops when a blast reaches it (`ROBO_HITS`); the dribble and the chip are closed-form paths of `t`.
- **Safadi's books** on the desk are a 3-D stack (`stBookStack`) with the titles on the spines.
- **The X-ray** on Sami's desk is `stXrayFilm`; the light box on the wall keeps the set's own picture.
- Uses props from other scenes: the board games from scene 28's `props.js` (`cuGame`, `cuGameFloor`), so it must stay registered before this one.
