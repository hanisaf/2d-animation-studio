# Game Night

> Straight after [34 Game Over!](../scene34_game_over/README.md): family game night in the living room. Professor Safadi plays *Oligarchy* with Housam and Danny; Doctor Sami plays *Crazy Dolphins* with Jenna and Alma. *"Allow me to explain the rules—"* *"No, Professor. This time, allow ME to explain!"* The kids explain, the kids win: Danny's towers bankrupt the professor, and the doctor's "very quick reflexes" slap the wrong dolphins. As the afternoon turns to evening, Safadi nods off mid-strategy and Sami follows (*"Doctors recommend eight hours of sleep… I will do eight minutes…"*), the twins snoring head to head. The kids giggle and keep playing; Danny takes the professor's turn for him. *"What do you think they are dreaming about?"* Two big dream bubbles swell over the sofa… *To be continued…*

## The two games (as the kids explain them)

- **Oligarchy** (green box, gold rim): roll the dice and move round the board. If nobody owns a street, buy it. If somebody does, pay them. Build towers and everyone who lands there pays more. Danny is ruthless at it.
- **Crazy Dolphins** (blue box): take turns flipping cards onto a pile. When two dolphins of the same colour come up in a row, slap the pile and shout *"CRAZY DOLPHINS!"* The first hand on the pile wins it. Slap the wrong colours and you give away two cards.

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`, `props.js`)

- **Timing constants** at the top of `scene.js`: `DEMO`, `ROLL1`, `SLAP1`, `SLAP2`, `HIGH5`, `DUSK`, `S_SLEEP`, `DICE_DROP`, `SNORE_S`, `M_YAWN`, `M_SLEEP`, `LEAN`, `GIGGLE`, `ROLL2`, `PAY2`, `SLAP3`, `GUESS`, `DREAM`, `FREEZE`, `END`. Dialogue times live in `asset.js`; move a line there and its beat here together.
- **Blocking (sofa view, +X is screen-left):** the twins sit on the front edge of the sofa cushion with their legs showing (Safadi X 95, Sami X −95, Z 140, hips at seat height, knees bent via `footL`/`footR`), not sunk and clipped as in scenes 33–34. The kids stand at the ends of the coffee table: Housam (200, 205) and Danny (290, 400) by Oligarchy, Alma (−200, 205) and Jenna (−290, 400) by Crazy Dolphins. The set's `front()` is interleaved so the table sits between the back and front pairs, and `gnTableGames` paints both games on its top.
- **Evening:** `dusk(t)` ramps the set's `night` and `lamp` over `DUSK` and dims the frame with a soft purple tint.
- **The inserts** (`props.js`): `gnOligarchy` (board, tokens, towers, money piles, dice, payments) and `gnDolphins` (stacks, the pile, flips and slaps) take their beats as options, so retiming a roll or a slap is one number in `scene.js`. Lines spoken during an insert get a bubble whose tail points to where the speaker sits (`OFF`).
- **Bubbles:** `gnZzz` for the snores, `gnThought` for the kids' guesses (`gnJuice`, `gnLecture`, `gnRobot`) and the twins' unrevealed dreams (`gnDream`).
