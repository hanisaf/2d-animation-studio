## Synopsis

Sami welcomes Alma and Housam and agrees to bring Jenna and Danny to the show. He gets everyone started. Roudaynah waves goodbye to Jenna and Danny. Jenna smiles at Alma and asks, “Should we get Derp and Gummy Blob married?”, then makes a funny face. Danny shows Housam his Nintendo Switch and says, “Let’s play Mario Kart”. Sami and Professor Safadi jump in to remind the kids to balance games with activities, take breaks, and put screens away at bedtime. Sarah frowns because she cannot come. Danny sets the Switch on a low stand, then shares a secret handshake with Housam. Roudaynah and Sarah walk away together while the cousins stay with Sami.
