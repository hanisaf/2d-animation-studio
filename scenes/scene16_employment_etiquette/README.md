# The Etiquette of Employment

A workplace satire in six beats. Dr. Sami politely asks to transfer clinics. Dr. P answers with an ominous threat, which turns out to be an absurdly oversized employment-etiquette textbook delivered by drone. The comedy targets bureaucratic control and the disproportionate response to an ordinary request. Dr. P is a fictional cartoon character. Duration: **42 seconds**.

## Script and shots

| Time | Shot / action |
| --- | --- |
| 0–6 s | At home, Dr. Sami holds a phone to his ear: “May I transfer clinics?” |
| 6–11 s | Cut to Dr. P on the phone in his office: “Don’t you dare, or else.” |
| 11–15 s | Back at home, Sami raises one eyebrow: “Or else what?” |
| 15–22 s | Dr. P maintains his officious expression: “Or else you’ll see something floating above your house.” |
| 22–28 s | Sami steps through his front door, phone still at his ear. The camera reveals a drone hovering above his roof. Sami looks up. No extra dialogue. |
| 28–36 s | The drone releases **one massive textbook**, initially seen spine-on. The drone moves aside; a striped parachute opens and the book turns toward the viewer while gently descending into the front yard. The unloaded drone stays above the roof. |
| 36–42 s | Cut closer to the book, its parachute settling behind it. Hold the readable cover: **The Etiquette of Employment**. Sami remains on the phone, giving the delivery a dry, raised-eyebrow look. |

All movement is a deterministic function of time. Dialogue bubbles and synthesized cartoon voices share the manifest's timing. The ending stays silent apart from light instrumental music, leaving the book's title as the punchline. The book lands in the yard without hitting anyone.
