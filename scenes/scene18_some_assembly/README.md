# Scene 18: Some Assembly Required

> In the science room, Alma and Jenna bolt Robo-Safadi together for Halloween. Housam and Danny drop by: *trick* gets you broccoli, *treat* gets you candy. But when Alma presses the button, the robot flops. ## Synopsis

**The assembly.** After school in the science room. Alma ratchets a bolt on Robo-Safadi's hip with a giant wrench (CLANK, CLANK). Jenna, up on a step stool, lowers his big box head onto the neck socket: *"Head… going ON!"* CLUNK. The project is on the whiteboard: *PROJECT: ROBO-SAFADI · Halloween in 6 days!!*

**The boys drop by.** Housam and Danny come in through the door. Danny bumps Mr. Bones, who rattles. *"Whoa! What are you girls doing?"* *"Is that… Professor Safadi?!"* The girls explain: a special robot for Halloween. *"Meet… ROBO-SAFADI!"* *"What will he do on Halloween?"* *"Trick… or treat!"* Jenna lifts his right arm: *"Trick gets you… broccoli!"* Alma puts her lollipop in his left gripper: *"And treat gets you… candy!"* Everyone laughs.

**It won't switch on.** *"So… how does it work?"* The girls look at each other. Alma pokes the status panel on his chest: *"Um… you press this button…?"* A fizzle (bzzt…), then the robot flops: arms drop, head sags, antenna droops; the broccoli and lollipop CLONK to the floor. *"…Why isn't it doing anything?"*

**The interruption.** *"I can explain!"*, and the door swings open. Professor Safadi leans in next to Mr. Bones: *"I heard explain… and you know that is my job!"* *"What are you guys doing?"* Danny dashes across the room and shoves him back into the doorway. All four: **"It's a SECRET!"** Safadi rubs his chin, *"Hmm… a secret?"*, shrugs, and leaves; the door shuts. Phew.

**Housam explains.** He hops over to the rolling whiteboard and spins it from the girls' blueprint to a clean side. He draws a robot: *"What you're creating is the **hardware**."* Then a `{ }`: *"You need the **software** for the robot to work."* *"Software?"* He wipes the board: **SOFTWARE = KNOWLEDGE**. *"It's what makes the robot **sense** the world…"* (an eye) *"…and decide how to **behave**."* (a light bulb, a gripper: SENSE → THINK → ACT).

**The promise.** Alma wilts: *"But… I don't know how to make software."* Housam wipes the board again: **PROGRAMMING**. *"Creating software is called programming."* He writes the robot's first program as he talks: `if (trick) { give(broccoli); } else { give(candy); }`. *"Don't worry! Danny and I will help you. We know how to program!"* *"Yeah!"* *"HOORAY!"*

**The tag.** Close on the slumped robot. His eyes blip once, twice… then light up cyan and the antenna perks up. *blip?* Freeze. **TO BE CONTINUED…**

**Theme:** a machine is only half a robot. The body is the hardware; what it knows and how it behaves is the software, and anyone can learn to write it.

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`)

- **Blocking** is in `POS`: Danny (−420, 925), Housam (−330, 900) and later by the board (−255, 945), the rolling whiteboard (−110, 1010), Alma (175, 960), Robo-Safadi (240, 990), Jenna on her stool (292, 1012, feet at Y 34). The boys' entrance paths are `D_PATH` / `H_PATH` from `DOOR`; `along()` walks a path with a dribble-cycle stride, and they fade in at the door.
- **Timing constants:** `CLANKS`, `HEAD_ON` (the head seats), `WALK_D` / `WALK_H`, `BUMP` (Mr. Bones), `ARM_R` / `ARM_L` (broccoli / lollipop), `LAUGH`, `PRESS` / `FLOP`, the interruption (`INTR`, `SAF_IN`, `DASH`, `SHOVE`, `SECRET`, `LEAVE`, `BACK_D`), `TO_EASEL`, `FLIP` (the board spins), `WIPES` (the eraser sweeps), `HOORAY`, `BLIP`, `FREEZE`.
- **The robot** is drawn by `drawRobot`. Until `HEAD_ON` it is drawn twice and clipped at the neck (local y −17): the body with a socket and two loose wires, and the head lifted and offset by `headOff(t)`. `robotPose` keeps him unpowered (`eyes: 'off'`, `hum: false`) until the blip. `robotProps` draws the lollipop in his gripper and the broccoli and lollipop falling to the floor after the flop (`fallY`, with one bounce).
- **Hands that touch things:** `stage()` measures the robot first (`measure`), and each pose gets a `ctx.local(screenPoint)` that turns a robot anchor into the character's local hand target. That's how Jenna holds the head and lifts the arm, and how Alma places the lollipop and pokes the chest panel.
- **The interruption:** `interrupt(name, t, c, pose)` overrides every kid's acting while Safadi is at the door (`INTR`), so the main poses are untouched. Safadi stands in the doorway at Z 1232 and slides along X (`AT.safadi`); he is clipped at the near door jamb's screen x, so the wall hides him until he leans in. `openDoor` paints the doorway open onto the hall (the set draws it closed). Danny's dash and walk back are `DASH` / `BACK_D` to `GUARD`.
- **The set:** `rows: 2` (the front bench row is cleared), `bones` is fed a `kick` at `BUMP`, and the room's whiteboard shows the girls' notes (`wallBoard`).
- **Draw order:** every character and prop is an actor with a Z; they're sorted far to near and interleaved with `science_room.front(…, { splitZ, toZ })`, so benches, the demo bench and Mr. Bones layer correctly.
