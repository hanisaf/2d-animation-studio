# Jenna Teaches Danny Division

At Meadow Ridge Elementary, **Jenna (4th grade)** teaches **Danny (1st grade)** division in two ways. She first shares four toy cars equally. Danny looks a little sad when he wants to play, then brightens when he answers correctly. Jenna sets the cars aside and introduces **45 ÷ 9** on her whiteboard. Danny is intimidated until a candy example shows five repeated subtractions of nine. He claps, explains the answer, gets distracted by the thought of candy, and summarizes both ways of understanding division. He then independently solves **14 ÷ 2 = 7** and earns playtime and candies. Jenna closes by promising something new next time, winking, and showing **Fafy, her toy giraffe**. Duration: **235 seconds (3:55)**.
