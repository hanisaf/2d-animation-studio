## Synopsis

The first scene of the **Pokemon** series. Housam and Alma stand on the soccer-pitch rug with their binder and cards spread out. Housam has pulled a shiny dragon, Alma a superhero dolphin. There's a knock, the door swings open, and Jenna and Danny walk in. *"Are those… stickers?"* *"No, silly! They are Pokemon cards!"* Housam says the cards have made 34 billion dollars, and Danny can't believe it. Jenna asks how cardboard can make that much money. Housam: *"Allow me to explain!"* Alma: *"You sound just like the Professor…"*

Then they give five reasons, taken from Housam's article *The Success of the Pokemon TCG*. Each reason gets a full-frame insert:

1. **The creatures are cute and cool.** Who doesn't want a superhero dolphin or a giant red dragon? Kids and grown-ups both love them, more than other card games.
2. **The art is amazing.** Some cards look better than modern art. A holo card hangs in a gallery between "Red Square" and "Line".
3. **It's a game, too.** You build a 60-card deck and battle. Starting is easy: plan a deck online and buy the cards for less than a dollar each. *BOOM!*
4. **Promo cards.** You can't pull them from a pack. You only get them in special boxes or at events, so fans buy the box.
5. **A huge market.** Every set has a few cards everybody wants, and people sell them. *"So cards are like treasure!"*

A whiteboard recap ticks off all five reasons: *= $34 BILLION!* Alma and Housam give Jenna and Danny their first cards (a Sproutbun and a Starpaw). *"I want my own collection!"* *"I want to build a deck and battle!"* *"Then let's all enter a Pokemon TCG tournament!"* *"LET'S DO IT!"* Freeze. *To be continued…*

The cards show the studio's own creatures (Captain Fin, Blazewyrm, Sproutbun and Starpaw), not real Pokemon art. Pokemon itself is only named in the dialogue.

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`, `props.js`)

- **Marks** (`MARK`): Danny (−195, 505), Jenna (−80, 485), Alma (40, 455), Housam (150, 465), left to right on the rug in the bed view. Jenna and Danny only appear in the bedroom shots after `ARRIVE` (18.4).
- **Cards in hand:** `HOLD` gives each kid's chest and raised hand height (local units), and the card width. `kidPose` returns a `card` field (`{ hand: 'L' | 'R' | 'mid', up }`), which `heldCard` draws at that hand. Jenna's and Danny's cards pop in at `GIFT` (117.2).
- **Bubbles:** in the room shots each balloon goes away from a neighbor standing close by. A shot can fix a speaker's side with its `sides` map (Alma's goes left in `experts`). In inserts the speaker is off screen and the tail points to their corner (`INS_SIDE`).
- **Reasons:** `R` holds the start of each reason, and `TICKS` holds the moment each checkmark lands in the recap. Both are used by the whiteboard on Housam's wall (`board`) and by the `summary` insert.
- **Props** (`props.js`, namespace `PK`): `PK.card(cx, cy, w, ang, { kind, holo, back, promo, t })` with `kind` one of `fin`, `dragon`, `sprout`, `star`; `PK.cardFloor` and `PK.binder` lie flat on a perspective floor; `PK.ins*` are the full-frame inserts, each taking its start time.
- **Source:** the five reasons and the figures (34 billion dollars in lifetime revenue, 60-card decks, cards for less than a dollar, promo cards in special boxes and at events, the resale market) come from Housam's article *The Success of the Pokemon TCG*.
