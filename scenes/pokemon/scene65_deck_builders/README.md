## Synopsis

The second scene of the **Pokemon** series, cutting between two rooms.

**Alma's bedroom.** Jenna and Danny stand on the rug next to a heap of cards. Jenna wants the cutest Pokemon (Sproutbun's little ears, Starpaw's star) and Danny wants the fiercest (Blazewyrm: *"RAWR!"*). Each card goes onto their pile with an *"In!"* A Fire Energy has no monster on it (*"Boring!"*), and Trainers (Stadiums, Tools) have no claws and no cuteness, so they sweep the whole heap of Energy and Trainer cards over their shoulders: *"USELESS!"* An insert shows the result: 60 Pokemon, 0 Energy, 0 Trainers. *"Our decks are perfect!"*

**Housam's bedroom.** Housam and Alma talk strategy: Blazewyrm has 180 HP but Fire is weak to Water (insert: Blazewyrm vs Captain Fin, WEAKNESS ×2). Housam answers with a Stadium (Lava Arena), Alma with a Tool (Bubble Shield). Then come Supporters to draw, Items to search, twelve Water Energy, four copies of the best Pokemon and *"sixty cards exactly!"* Their deck plan is on the whiteboard.

**Professor Safadi drops in** on Jenna and Danny. *"Sixty Pokemon, and no Energy? No Trainers?"* *"Allow me to explain!"* At his chalkboard he gives the best practices:
1. A deck is a team. Every card needs a job.
2. About **16 Pokemon, 12 Energy and 32 Trainers** (= 60).
3. No Energy means no attacks.
4. Trainers draw cards, find your Pokemon and change the battle.
5. Pick one or two types, with Energy to match.
6. Play four copies of your best cards.
7. Have a plan for your weakness.

*"But Energy cards aren't cute…"* *"And Trainers aren't scary!"* *"We're sticking to our plan!"* Safadi sighs, then pulls one extra card out of his jacket: a **Professor Safadi** Supporter card (*"He explains. You win."*). *"Trust me… you will need me in the battle!"* He tosses it onto Jenna's pile and everybody laughs.

**Doctor Sami checks on Housam and Alma.** They brag that their deck is perfectly balanced and unbeatable, and Sami is dazzled: *"That's more planning than a heart surgery!"* He offers a **Doctor Sami** Trainer card (*"Heal all your Pokemon."*). They laugh: *"No thanks, Doctor Sami!"* *"Our deck is full!"* *"Tough crowd…"*

**The living room.** The brothers walk in from opposite sides. *"Brother, I think we are going to witness a heated battle."* *"I agree."* *"Who do you think will win?"* *"We shall see!"* They laugh. Freeze. *To be continued…*

The Pokemon on the cards are the studio's own creatures (scene 64). The Trainer names (Lava Arena, Bubble Shield, Deck Coach, Super Search) are made up.

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`, `props.js`)

- **Rooms:** `room(t)` picks the set from the part boundaries `P2`, `P3`, `P4` and `P5`. `MARK` holds each room's blocking, and `present()` decides who is in a room (Safadi from `SAF_IN`, Sami from `SAMI_IN`).
- **Acting:** each character has one pose function (`jennaPose`, `dannyPose`, `expertPose`, `safadiPose`, `samiPose`), keyed to dialogue times. `card` / `card2` in a pose put a card in the right or left hand (`up` holds it beside the head). `EXPERT_CARD` maps each of Housam's and Alma's lines to the card they show.
- **Flying cards:** `FLY` lists every card thrown in Alma's room (start time, duration, world-space start, floor landing spot, arc height). A card lies on the floor once it has landed, so the piles fill in up to the end of the scene.
- **Props** (`props.js`, namespace `DK`): `DK.card(cx, cy, w, ang, { kind })` draws any card. Kinds are `fireE`, `waterE`, `stadium`, `tool`, `supporter` and `item`, plus `safadi` and `sami`, whose art window draws the real rig at screen scale. Creature kinds go to scene 64's `PK.card`. The inserts are `DK.insDecks`, `insMatch`, `insChalk` (its `BEATS` are the line start times) and `insCard`. `DK.banner` draws the room captions.
- **Deck numbers:** a 16 / 12 / 32 split is a common starting point for a 60-card Pokemon TCG deck. Most decks run about 12–20 Pokemon, 8–14 Energy and 25–35 Trainers, with at most four copies of any card except basic Energy.
