## Synopsis

The third scene of the **Pokemon** series, straight after scene 65's "Who do you think will win?"

**Ready.** The four kids stand at the ends of two playmats on the living-room rug: Jenna against Alma on the left, Danny against Housam on the right. *"LET'S BATTLE!"* Their active Pokemon light up as holograms over the cards: Sproutbun against Captain Fin, and Blazewyrm against Blazewyrm.

**The attacks.** Each attack gets a full-frame battle insert with a move name, HP bars, an anticipation crouch, the effect, a hit flash, screen shake, the damage number and KNOCKED OUT!:
1. **Tidal Splash** (Alma): Captain Fin surfs a curling wave onto Sproutbun. *"Sproutbun, no! My turn… um… attack?"* *"You need Energy to attack, Jenna."* *"Oh… I pass."*
2. **Flame Burst** (Housam, with the Lava Arena Stadium, +30): a fireball across a volcano arena. *"Fight back, Blazewyrm! Why isn't it moving?"* *"No Energy, no attacks."* *"…Pass."*
3. **Bubble Beam** (Alma): a stream of bubbles knocks out Starpaw.
4. **Inferno Dive** (Housam): Blazewyrm flies off the top of the frame and dives back down in a trail of fire. Big shake.

A montage keeps the knockouts coming until both experts have one prize card left. Jenna and Danny just stand and watch (*cricket*).

**The last card.** *"Wait! I still have one card left…"* *"Me too! We play…"* *"PROFESSOR SAFADI!"* In the insert, the card flips in, a giant holographic professor rises with glowing eyes and chalk formulas (*"Allow me to explain… you win!"*), and Captain Fin and Blazewyrm get dizzy and fall over. JENNA WINS! DANNY WINS! *"WE WIN!"*

**The argument.** *"That's not fair!"* *"One card can't just win the game!"* *"Battling your super decks wasn't fair!"* *"Everybody had the same chance to build a deck!"* It ends with *"NOT FAIR!"* against *"TOTALLY FAIR!"*

**The brothers** walk in through the archway. *"Children! What is going on here?"* Housam and Alma explain. Sami: the only way to cancel a Professor Safadi card is a **Doctor Sami** card (he holds one up), *"but someone was too proud to take it."* Alma: *"…Oops."*

**The notebook.** Housam thinks and opens his notebook (insert: the POKEDEX page ticks off Captain Fin, Blazewyrm, Sproutbun and Starpaw, while Professor Safadi and Doctor Sami are NOT FOUND!). *"These cards are not official! They are not in the Pokedex!"* BUSTED! The brothers sweat: *"Well… technically… ahem…"* *"They are… limited edition!"* *"Very, very limited."* Housam and Alma point at them: *"Unofficial cards! WE WIN!"* Jenna and Danny: *"No! WE win!"*

**Everybody wins.** *"Everybody wins this time!"* *"BUT HOW?!"* *"You shall see!"* The brothers wink. Freeze. *To be continued…*

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`, `props.js`)

- **Blocking** (`MARK`, TV view): Jenna (−420, 470), Alma (−190, 470), Danny (−50, 470) and Housam (180, 470) stand in a row at the ends of mat A (`MAT.A`, −305) and mat B (`MAT.B`, 65). The brothers walk in from the archway (`FROM`) to (300, 400) and (400, 360), on the right and out of the kids' way.
- **Battle state:** `J_ACT` and `D_ACT` list when Jenna's and Danny's active Pokemon are knocked out (always inside an insert) and when the next one is played. Prize counts follow from the knockouts; after `AFTER` (the montage) the experts are down to 1. `ENERGY` lists Alma's and Housam's Energy attaches (each makes their hologram hop), and `STADIUM` is when the Lava Arena card goes down and mat B glows.
- **Holograms:** `PB.holo(P, x, z, kind, flip, t, { k, ko, dizzy, happy })` draws a creature from `PK.ART` standing over its card, with a projector glow, a light cone and scanlines. They switch off when the brothers walk in.
- **Attack inserts:** `PB.insAttack(t, t0, { atk, def, move, fx, dmg, ko, stadium })` with `fx` one of `wave`, `fireball`, `bubbles` or `dive`. The hit lands at `t0 + 2.0`, and KNOCKED OUT! shows at `t0 + 3.1`. Add a new move by adding an `fx` branch.
- **Other inserts:** `PB.insMontage(t, t0, kos)`, `PB.insSafadi(t, t0)` (the card flips at 0.3 s, the professor rises at 0.8–2.0 s, the Pokemon fall at 3.0–3.6 s, the WINS! stamps at 3.7 s) and `PB.insNotebook(t, t0)`. `PB.notebook` is the small book in Housam's hands.
- **Unofficial on purpose:** the Professor Safadi and Doctor Sami cards are the brothers' homemade cards (scene 65). The real game has no such cards, which is what the notebook shows.
