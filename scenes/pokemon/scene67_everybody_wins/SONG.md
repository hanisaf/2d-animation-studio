# "Everybody Wins": the song script for Gemini

Paste **Part A** into Gemini as the style prompt, then **Part B** as the lyrics. Words in `[square brackets]` are section and performance tags, and words in `(parentheses)` are backing vocals, crowd chants or shouts.

**Done:** the recording came back at about 134 BPM, with different section lengths than this plan. The scene is now cut to the recording (see `lyrics.js` and the README). The bar map below is the original plan.

## Part A: style prompt

> A 3-minute epic, cinematic kids' anthem for the grand finale of a cartoon monster-battle tournament. 128 BPM, 4/4, key of D major, with a key change up to E major for the final chorus. It's a stadium anthem crossed with a video-game boss-battle theme and a film-score finale. Instruments: a full orchestra (big brass, timpani, soaring strings, a choir singing "oh-oh" and "ah"), an electric guitar, a synth bass, a huge drum kit with stadium stomps and claps, and chiptune arpeggios in the battle sections. Arena sounds are part of the arrangement: a cheering crowd, a stadium announcer echo, whooshes into each battle, a riser and a 3-2-1 countdown into a massive beat drop, a moment of near-silence with a music box, and fireworks at the end.
>
> It is sung by a cast of cartoon characters, each with a distinct voice:
> - **Doctor Sami**: a goofy, bouncy, playful announcer tenor
> - **Professor Safadi**: a warm, formal, proud baritone; dramatic and announcer-like
> - **The kids** (Housam, Alma, Jenna, Danny): a bright, cheeky children's choir that shouts the hooks
> - **Housam**: a quick, rhythmic, confident boy rapper
> - **Jenna**: a sweet, determined girl
> - **Danny**: an excited, loud little boy
> - **Princess Pearl**: a deadpan, regal diva who turns sincere and soaring for her solo
> - **Neptune**: a booming, royal, theatrical king
> - **Jester Fester**: a cheerful, sing-song jester
> - **Dragon**: a gentle, low, sweet voice that sneezes
>
> Structure: an orchestral intro with a crowd and a spoken welcome, a roll-call verse that names one trainer and their monster per bar, a pre-chorus that builds, a huge chant-along chorus ("Battle on!"), a rap verse, a second pre-chorus, a second chorus, a half-time piano-and-strings bridge with a diva solo, a 3-2-1 build, a massive instrumental drop with "BATTLE ON! HEY!" chants, a near-silent breakdown, a key-changed final chorus ("Everybody wins!") with names chanted over it, and a big celebratory outro with fireworks and a final chord. The hooks "Battle on!" and "Everybody wins!" must be extremely catchy and easy to chant.

## When the song comes back

1. Save it as `audio.mp3` in this folder and add `audio: 'audio.mp3'` to `asset.js`.
2. Measure the BPM, the first downbeat and each section's start in the recording. Then update `SONG` (bpm, beat0) and `S` (the section starts) at the top of `scene.js`. Every shot is placed from those, so the cut follows.
3. Optional: a `lyrics.js` with word times, like scene 22's, to drive karaoke lines and lip sync. For now, the mouths of whoever is on screen flap on a generic pattern.
