# Palm Pals Adventures: The Adventure

> **180 seconds (3:00) · 1920 × 1080 · 24 fps · Series: Sami's Shorts (Sami's studio)**  
> Animated adaptation of Jenna Safadi's 8-page comic book [The Adventure](../../../comics/palm_pals_adventures/the_adventure/README.md) (*Palm Pals Adventures #1*).

## Synopsis

Ten-year-old **Eve Earth** wakes up to the smell of Dad's sizzling pancakes, sneaks an irresistible bite straight off the stove, and gets caught in a pancake-heaven daydream just as the doorbell rings. In walks his worst "enemy" — bubbly six-year-old **Zelie Pink Lemonade** (*"Just call me Zelie"*) — and her tall coral-red **Papa**. After a stormy rivalry showdown and a warp-speed breakfast, Eve sneaks to his room to test a mysterious portal button. Zelie barges in and leaps straight into the swirling blue vortex! Forced to rescue her, Eve dives in too, popping out in Adventure World wearing a brown plaid explorer hat as the portal locks behind a wooden sign: *"Find the key to open portal."* Ten chaotic hours (and one cartoon bear chase!) later, they burst out laughing, make up, and realize the golden key was sitting under Eve's itchy hat the whole time. Back home they immediately land in **Time Out** — only to become best friends anyway!

## Cast

- **Zelie Pink Lemonade** (`palm_pals` · `'zelie'`): 6-year-old pink lemonade cup plushie with white whipped foam rim, pink-striped straw, yellow lemon slice, and cute stubby pink hands & feet.
- **Eve's Dad** (`palm_pals` · `'eve_dad'`): Larger deep-blue Earth plushie with darker green continents, cute stubby blue hands & feet, and his signature dark wavy squiggly mustache.
- **Zelie's Papa** (`palm_pals` · `'zelie_papa'`): Taller coral-red fruit punch cup plushie with cream foam rim, yellow straw, cute stubby red hands & feet, and his signature dark wavy squiggly mustache.
