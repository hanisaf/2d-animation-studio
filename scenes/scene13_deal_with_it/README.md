## Synopsis

**The lecture.** A sunny afternoon at the county field. Professor Safadi has rolled a floating chalkboard onto the centre circle and is teaching Housam and Alma *how to play soccer*. His powers draw it all out: a triangle of **passing** lanes, a zig-zag **dribbling** path through cones, a **shooting** angle into the top corner, and finally a ring of stick figures joined by arrows for **teamwork**. Housam, who has scored more goals than anyone in the county, keeps a ball up on his knee the whole time. Alma tries very hard to stay awake.

**The challenge.** Housam lets the ball drop. *"Professor… soccer is a sport. You can't lecture about it."* He flicks the ball to Safadi: *"Let's play. You against us."* Safadi traps it (badly), then smiles a slow, knowing smile.

**Two against two.** Safadi cups his hands: *"SAMI!"* From behind the red team shelter steps Doctor Sami, stethoscope and all, identical down to the eyebrows. Housam and Alma do a double take: two Safadis. *"Kids… meet my brother."* *"Hi! I'm Doctor Sami!"* *"Two against two."* *"Let's see who wins!"* Alma: *"No fair! Adults against kids!"* The twins slide on matching sunglasses in perfect sync: *"Deal with it."*

**Four against two.** Housam grins. He puts two fingers in his mouth and whistles. Jenna cartwheels in from the sideline; Danny comes dribbling in with his own ball. *"Did somebody say SOCCER?!"* The four kids line up. Safadi lowers his sunglasses: *"Four against two? That's not fair!"* Danny holds up four pixel-block sunglasses (Minecraft style): *"Here!"* They fly onto each kid's face, pop, pop, pop, pop: **"DEAL WITH IT!"**

**The referee.** A whistle shrieks. Jester Fester jogs into the middle in a black-and-white striped referee shirt over his jester outfit, cap bells jingling, holding a ball under his arm. *"Did somebody say… UNFAIR? Then you need a REFEREE!"* He places the ball on the centre spot, raises the whistle and blows: **TWEEEET!** Freeze frame. **TO BE CONTINUED…**

**Theme:** you learn a sport by playing it, not by listening to a lecture. And everybody, adults included, thinks "fair" means "whatever helps my team."

- **Key times** are constants at the top of `scene.js`: `DROP` (ball down), `FLICK`, `SHIN`, `SHADES`, `LOWER`, `FWEET`, `CART`, `DRIB`, `PIX` (when each pair of pixel shades lands), `REF_WHISTLE`, `JOG`, `PLACE`, `TWEET`, `FREEZE`. Lines live in `asset.js`.
- **Group lines:** a dialogue entry with `group: [...]` draws one bubble over everyone in the group; the others' copies are `silentBubble`, so they still lip-flap and voice.
- **Blocking:** `AT` gives each character's floor point; `SAM_T`, `JEN_T`, `DAN_T`, `FES_T` are their entrances, and `SHOWN` hides them before they arrive.
- **The ball:** juggled from Housam's knee anchor until `DROP`, then a world-space path (`ballWorld`) that crosses the cut to Safadi's shin. Danny's and Fester's balls are separate props.
