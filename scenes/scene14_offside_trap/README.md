# Scene 14: The Offside Trap

> The first half of Kids vs. Adults. The Safadi twins whisper a plan: the **offside trap**. Danny outruns Safadi and scores past a snoozing Sami: offside. Alma makes a miracle save, the kids pass it up the field, and Housam scores with a backflip bicycle kick: offside again. *"WHY???"* Referee Fester explains offside Safadi-style until Safadi cuts him off: *"Explanation is MY business, Jester."* Fester shrugs: *"Anyway… it's half-time!"* 0–0. *To be continued…*

## How the trap works (so the calls are right)

With only two players, the Adults' goalkeeper (Sami) is always the **last** defender and Safadi is always the **second-to-last**. Safadi never comes back to defend: he loafs around the halfway line or deeper in the Kids' half. So whenever a kid in the Adults' half is passed the ball forward, there is only *one* opponent (Sami) between them and the goal line, and they are **offside**. That is the twins' whole plan, and it's why both goals are flagged. It is also why Fester's explanation boils down to *"two opponents"*.

- **Goal 1:** Housam passes forward to Danny, who is already sprinting past Safadi into the Adults' half. Offside.
- **Goal 2:** Danny passes forward to Housam, who is ahead of him near the box, with Safadi stranded back in the Kids' half after his failed attack. Offside.

## Synopsis

**The huddle.** Kick-off position. Fester, in his striped referee shirt, holds the ball at the centre spot. On the Adults' side, Safadi and Sami huddle and whisper. Safadi's chalkboard pops up for a second, just for Sami: two little X's, a long arrow, and the words *OFFSIDE TRAP*. *"Brother… you guard the goal. I stay up front."* *"And I'll… guard it very… relaxingly."* They break the huddle with a sly *"heh heh heh."* The kids line up: Housam and Danny up front, Jenna in defence, Alma in goal in big gloves. Fester blows: kick-off.

**Goal 1.** The Kids kick off. Housam dribbles; Safadi lumbers toward him, arms out, *"Ah-ha!"*; Housam slides a pass forward and Danny is already off, a blur of little legs past Safadi. Safadi lunges at the ball, misses, and spins in a circle. Danny races into the box, and Sami is asleep against the post, snoring, a bubble rising from his nose. Danny shoots. The ball rolls past Sami's sneakers into the net. **"GOAAAL!"** The kids pile on each other, Danny does his goofy dance, Alma dances in her goal.

**Not for long.** A whistle shrieks. Fester jogs over waggling his finger: *"No, no, no! That doesn't count. OFFSIDE!"* The kids freeze mid-celebration. Sami wakes up with a snort: *"Huh? Did we win?"* The twins chuckle: *"heh heh heh."*

**The miracle save.** Safadi gets the ball and charges up the field, tie flapping, knees high, sloppy but determined. Jenna tries a tackle and he stumbles past her more by luck than skill. *"Professor Safadi… SHOOTS!"* (he narrates himself). A blasting shot at the top corner. Alma flies sideways, full stretch, sparkles trailing, and punches it away. **"WHAAAT?!"** Safadi drops to his knees, hands on his head. Alma: *"Not in MY goal."*

**The passing move.** Alma rolls it out to Jenna. Jenna cartwheels forward with the ball and passes to Danny. Danny dribbles, taps it on to Housam, who is in the box. Housam flicks it up, does a full backflip and hits a bicycle kick on the way round. Sami dives the wrong way (and lands on his face). **"GOAAAL!"** Even the fans in the bleachers stand up.

**Offside again.** The whistle. Fester, finger up: *"Offside! Again!"* All four kids, together: **"WHY???"**

**The explanation.** Fester steps forward and straightens his collar. *"Allow me to explain… Safadi-style!"* He strikes Safadi's pose, wiggles his fingers, and a tiny, slightly crooked chalkboard sputters into the air beside him. *"When the ball is passed forward to you, you need TWO opponents between you and their goal line. One… the goalkeeper."* (a chalk X for Sami) *"Two… usually a defender. But their only other player is…"* Everyone turns. Safadi, far up the pitch, waves. *"…way over there! So you only had ONE. Offside!"* The kids: *"Ohhhh!"* Fester beams: *"And that's why we call it the off—"*

**The cut-off.** Safadi steps in front of him, and Fester's board pops like a soap bubble. *"Ahem. Explanation is MY business, Jester."* Fester looks at the camera, shrugs: *"Anyway… it's half-time!"* He blows the whistle. The scoreboard: KIDS 0 – ADULTS 0, HALF TIME. Freeze. *To be continued…*

**Theme:** a rule can feel unfair until someone explains it, and the kids learn the rule the adults are using against them, which sets up their comeback in the second half.

## Notes for editing

- **Key times** are constants at the top of `scene.js` (`KICKOFF`, `PASS1`, `LUNGE`, `SHOT1`, `GOAL1`, `WHISTLE1`, `LONG`, `STUMBLE`, `SHOT2`, `SAVE`, `ROLL`, `CART`, `PASS3`, `PASS4`, `FLICK`, `FLIP`, `VOLLEY`, `GOAL2`, `WHISTLE2`, `BOARD_ON`, `ZOOM_IN`, `BOARD_POP`, `HALF`, `FREEZE`). Lines live in `asset.js`.
- **Blocking:** `PATHS` holds `[t, x, z]` keys per character, linear between keys (so walk and run cycles stay in step); a repeated time is a teleport across a cut. Gaits come from each rig's walk/skip/dribble helper, driven by distance travelled.
- **The ball:** `BALL` is a list of segments: resting, at someone's feet, a pass or shot (with an arc) from wherever it was, or held. A pass's start point is wherever the previous segment left it, so moving a player moves the ball with them.
- **Eyelines:** `group()` projects everyone first, so `ctx.look(name)` and `ctx.ball` turn heads toward a speaker or the ball in either camera direction.
- **New in this scene:** the `offside` kind for `safadiBoard` (Fester's board and the twins' huddle board); Alma's gloves, Sami's nose bubble, the cartwheel and backflip spins are scene props.
- **Set:** `crowd` .45; the fans cheer on both goals and the save; scoreboard `clock: 'HALF TIME'` in the last shot.
