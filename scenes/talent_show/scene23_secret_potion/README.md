# Scene 23: The Secret Potion

> The kids meet in the DCES gym. Jenna practices there every day, and she shows Alma her cartwheels. Alma tries one and flops: *"I want to do a cartwheel just like you, Jenna!"* Housam teases Professor Safadi: is there a *book* for that? Safadi takes the question seriously. **Explicit knowledge** is the *what*, and it lives in books (want countries and capitals? pick up an atlas). **Tacit knowledge** is the *how*: it lives inside you and only practice teaches it. So Alma practices with Jenna. Meanwhile Housam dribbles, flips and bicycle-kicks a ball into the hoop. Danny begs to learn, and Housam answers Safadi-style: soccer skills are tacit, so practice with me. Danny wants a shortcut: *"I want a secret potion that gives me soccer super skills!"* The Safadi brothers wink. A *healthy* potion: **broccoli juice with unsweet tea**. Danny drinks it (*BLEEECH!*); Housam sees his face and would rather practice. For a week Alma and Jenna cartwheel, Housam drills, and Danny sits on the bench with his Rubik's cube and his potion. On Friday Alma lands a cartwheel. *"Nothing beats watching the kids grow their skills."* *"…and drinking broccoli juice with unsweet tea!"* *To be continued…*
