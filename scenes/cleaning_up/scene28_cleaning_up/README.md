## Synopsis

An autumn afternoon in front of Alma's house. Jenna, Danny and Housam bounce on the lawn, each holding a board game: Jenna's *Rainbow Race*, Danny's *Crazy Dolphins* and Housam's *Oligarchy*. They can't wait for the play date. *"LET US GO!"* They skip up the walk and in through the front door.

Upstairs, Alma is dancing in a bedroom where the toys are all over the place: blocks, books, plush Pearl and Neptune, the Jester and Robo-Safadi figures, socks and crayons. The kids freeze. Housam asks Alma to clean it up; *"Not now,"* she says, and goes back to dancing. Danny says there's nowhere to open a board game. Jenna says let's all clean up together; Alma cheers *"HOORAY!"*; the boys refuse: *"No way! We did not mess it up!"*

Knock knock: Professor Safadi drops in. Housam explains, Alma says nobody wants to help her, and Safadi powers up an idea bulb: *"How about all of you collaborate?"* Jenna agrees, the boys don't (*"We are here to play!"*), so Safadi raises one finger: *"If you want to play in Alma's room… you have to help."* *"OK…"* The kids put their games down and start cleaning, the boys grumbling at first, and the mess starts to shrink.

Halfway through they run out of steam: *"We are tired… cleaning up is boring."* Safadi: *"Not everything in life is fun. Many boring tasks are necessary."* Alma thinks there must be another way, and Housam has one: hire someone! Danny and Jenna love it. But Safadi explains that if you clean up yourself you know where each toy is, and if you hire someone you lose track of them. *"Awwww…"* He tells them to keep working (*"I will be back later. I want the room to be cleaned up!"*) and leaves. The kids slump: a sigh, a yawn, crickets. Then Housam lights up with a bulb: *"EUREKA!"* The others stare at him, puzzled. Freeze. *To be continued…*

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`)

The dialogue times live in `asset.js`; move a line there and its beat in the pose functions together.
- **Blocking:** `LAWN` (house) and `SPOT` (bedroom) hold everyone's floor spot; `at(name, t)` walks them along paths and fades them through the front door and the bedroom doorway.
- **The mess:** `messy(t)` drives the bedroom's `messy` option: 1 until the clean-up, easing to .55 by the time they get tired, so toys disappear one by one while the kids work (and the room is only half done: to be continued).

- **After the clean-up:** `lateKid` drives all four kids from `TIRED` on (the slump, the hiring idea, "Awwww", watching Safadi leave, the yawn, the EUREKA and the puzzled faces). Jenna, Danny and Housam's rigs don't draw emotes, so `drawChar` draws theirs with Alma's `alEmote`.
