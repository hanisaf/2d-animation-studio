# Cleaning Up 2 · The Helping Hands

> Straight on from Housam's *"EUREKA!"*: Alma asks what eureka means. *"In old Greece…"* In Housam's story, Professor Safadi sits in a bubbly marble tub in a sunny Greek bathhouse, in a chiton and laurel wreath, feels around under the water and leaps up: *"EUREKA!"* Doctor Sami runs in: *"Brother, what did you find?"* *"The soap!"* Back in Alma's room, the kids burst out laughing.
>
> Then Housam's real idea: program Robo-Safadi to clean up, and let the LLM decide where the toys go. He fetches the robot and his laptop and types `llm("Clean up Alma's room")`. *"Let us hope it doesn't go haywire!"* *"Guaranteed! This is the new frontier model with many guardrails."* Clawd wakes (*"Boopy boop!"*), the robot's chest swings open and every toy in the room flies into it. *"ROOM CLEAN UP!"* *"But… my toys are gone!"* Professor Safadi comes back: *"Ahh, room cleaned! Wonderful job!"* *"But we have a small problem… Allow me to explain!"* *To be continued…*

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`)

- **Timing constants** at the top: `STORY`, `WAVE1` / `WAVE2` (the two ripple dissolves), `HUM`, `SEARCH`, `GRAB`, `BULB`, `EUREKA`, `SAMI_IN`, `SHOW` (the soap revealed), `SQUIRT` (it flies back into the tub), `LAUGH`, `END`. Dialogue times live in `asset.js`; move a line there and its beat here together.
- **The tub:** Safadi is drawn through the bath's `bather` option, so the set draws him between the far and near halves of the water (he sits *in* it, under the soap foam). `dy` sinks him; `jump` lifts him out.
- **The story look:** every bathhouse shot gets `storyLook()`, a warm storybook vignette, so it reads as Housam's tale.
- **Ripple dissolve:** `rippleShot([a, b], from, to)` paints both shots off-screen and lays them down in wavy, cross-faded strips. Reusable for any flashback.
- **The soap:** `soapBar()` draws it (in Safadi's hand when the pose has `soap: true`, then flying along `flyingSoap`).
- **Part 2 timing constants:** `P2` (part 2 starts), `H_OUT` / `H_IN` (Housam out and back), `R_IN` (the robot marches in), `PLUG`, `CODE_T` (typing), `RUN`, `UNPLUG`, `LID`, `SET_DOWN`, `CMD` (Alma's "Clean up!"), `POWER`, `HATCH`, `SUCK` (the toys fly), `CLOSE`, `SHING` (spotless), `OFF_T` (power down), `GRAB_G` (the boys grab their games), `SAF_IN`, `FREEZE`, `END`.
- **The robot takes the toys:** the bedroom's new `taken` option (0..1) removes every toy one by one, tidy places included; `BR.takeables()` lists where each toy was and when it goes (`ord`), and `flyingToys()` flies each one along an arc into Robo-Safadi's new `hatch` (his chest door; `A.hatch` is the target). `roomSet(t)` keeps the room at `messy: .55` until `SHING`, then snaps it spotless (`messy: 0`, `taken: 1`).
- **The laptop:** held open at Housam's chest (`heldLaptop`, pose `laptop: true`, or `'closed'`), closed on the floor from `SET_DOWN` (`floorLaptop`); `cable()` runs from it to the robot between `PLUG` and `UNPLUG`. The code and Clawd are shown full-frame with scene 19's `ieEditor`.
- **The code** says `if clean_up:` rather than `clean-up` (a hyphen isn't valid in Python). Change `CODE` to taste.
- Uses props from other scenes: the board games from scene 28's `props.js` (`cuGame`, `cuGameFloor`) and the laptop editor from scene 19's `props.js` (`ieEditor`), so both must stay registered before this one.
