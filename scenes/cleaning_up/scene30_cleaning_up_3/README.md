# Cleaning Up 3 · The Eureka Moment

> Straight on from *"Allow me to explain!"*: Housam tells Professor Safadi how the LLM-powered Robo-Safadi swallowed every toy in Alma's room. What did they learn? *"Never trust technology!"* Safadi corrects them: software must be **tested** before it goes to production, and they should have depended on themselves. *"But my toys are still gone!"* The robot won't respond; Safadi suspects hardware, and Housam finds the battery is flat. While it charges, Alma can't wait, so Safadi tells a story: *"In ancient Greece…"* In front of the Acropolis, Greek-robed children beg him to teach them physics, mathematics, astronomy. He answers: *"Gnōthi seauton."* Puzzled faces. *"Know thyself…"* The charger beeps. Back in the bedroom, Robo-Safadi is charged and pours the toys back out all over the floor. Alma is happy, until she sees the mess. This time the kids clean up themselves. *"After you clean up, I will continue the story about knowing oneself."* *"No way! After we clean up, we want to play board games!"* Safadi smiles and winks. *To be continued…*

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`)

- **Timing constants** at the top: `SET_G` (the boys put their games down), `BOARD` (chalkboard in, fully drawn, gone), `FIX_WALK`, `TAPS`, `INSERT`, `DOOR`, `TO_WALL`, `PLUG`, `BACK_WALK`, `WAVE1` / `WAVE2` (the ripples), `HUSH`, `GNOTHI`, `PUZZLE`, `KNOW`, `BEEP`, `WAKE`, `DUMP` / `D_FLY` (the hatch, then the toys' flights), `MESS_SEEN`, `CLEAN`, `STOP_CLEAN`, `GRAB_G`, `FREEZE`, `END`. Dialogue times live in `asset.js`; move a line there and its beat here together.
- **Housam's walk:** `at('housam', t)` takes him from his spot to the robot (`FIX`), to the wall socket (`WALL`), and back to his spot for the story.
- **The battery:** `battery(t)` drives both the robot's chest gauge and the insert: 3% until `PLUG`, filling while he charges, full after the story. The insert is `roboSafadiBatteryInsert`, since the rig has no back view. The cable runs from his hip port to `SOCKET` on the wall behind him.
- **The chalkboard** is Safadi's new `'testing'` board (`safadiBoard(…, { kind: 'testing' })`), placed on his right in the `R_LESSON` camera so it clears Alma.
- **Athens:** Safadi stands on the bema (`AT.BEMA`, Y 18); the students are `student` rigs at the set's five student spots. The reverse camera stands where he stands, so he isn't drawn when `P.dir < 0`. `glyphs()` writes ΓΝΩΘΙ ΣΕΑΥΤΟΝ above his head (or over the students in the reverse shot) and morphs it into KNOW THYSELF.
- **The dump:** `roomSet(t)` keeps the room spotless (`messy: 0, taken: 1`) until `D_FLY`, then runs `taken` from 1 back to 0 at `messy: .55`. `flyingToys()` flies each toy out of the hatch to its messy spot, landing just as it reappears in the room. During the clean-up, `messy` eases from .55 to .25, so toys go back to their tidy places one by one.
- **Clean-up cycle:** `pickPose` is scene 28's (crouch, grab, lift, toss every 1.5 s), cheerful this time, with a twinkle on each toss (`extras`).
- Uses props from other scenes: the board games from scene 28's `props.js` (`cuGame`, `cuGameFloor`), so it must stay registered before this one.
