## Synopsis

After school, Safadi stands on the entrance mark at DCES with a stack of books and announces that kids should read books, not play video games. Housam traps his soccer ball under his foot and says he disagrees. *"Then we have a debate! To the lecture hall!"* Safadi's power flares, a white flash fills the frame, and everyone is in the lecture hall.

Safadi and Housam stand at the front of the stage. The two front-wall chalkboards become the scoreboard, **BOOKS** on the left and **GAMES** on the right, and each point chalks itself onto its side. Alma, Fester and Dragon sit in row 1, and their heads turn to follow whoever is talking. Safadi says games are mindless, lonely and make kids dumber. Housam answers each point with his essay: the information load in *Breath of the Wild*, *Overcooked 2* and the study where teams that gamed for 45 minutes were 20% more productive, the 58-quadrillion-wrong-answers puzzle in *Phantom Hourglass*, and the working-memory study. Safadi scores one fair point: a link is not a cause. Housam concedes it and finishes with stress relief and "a game for everybody."

Safadi calls the vote. For books, nobody raises a hand except Safadi, and a cricket chirps. For video games, Alma raises her hand, Fester does jazz hands and Dragon flaps his wings; three tally marks appear on the GAMES board. Safadi is puzzled, then gives in: *"Fine. You can play video games."* The kids celebrate until he adds *"…Thirty minutes a day."* A record scratch stops the party, everyone groans *"Awww…"*, and Safadi chuckles as the scene irises out.

**Keep it fair.** Safadi is not a straw man. Housam wins by answering real concerns with evidence, and concedes the one point Safadi gets right.

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`)

- **Staging:** Safadi at (−250, 40, 1925) and Housam at (250, 40, 1925), at the front lip of the stage so they read well against the boards. The audience sits in row 1: Fester `seat(1, 175)`, Alma `seat(1, 0, 42)`, Dragon `seat(1, −195)`. In the reverse view, Safadi is screen-right and Housam screen-left, so `floorSide(t)` swings the audience's gaze toward whoever spoke last.
- **Scoreboard:** `BOOKS`, `GAMES` and `VOTES` at the top of `scene.js` list the chalk times. `scoreBoard()` draws them through `lectureHall`'s `boardL` / `boardR` hooks with a left-to-right write-on. The matching `plip` cues are in `asset.js`.
- **Bubbles:** the three extra "Awww…" entries (Housam, Fester, Dragon) are marked `silentBubble`, so their voices play but only Alma's bubble draws.
- **Timing constants:** `HALL_AT` (17.3), `TOSS` / `BONK`, `CHEER` (114) and `SCRATCH` (120.9) drive the poses; move them together with the matching cues in `asset.js`.
