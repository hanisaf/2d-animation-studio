## Synopsis

Safadi's slide reads *One thing at a time*. Attention, he says, is like a spotlight: it shines on one thing at a time, and if you try to do two things you do worse at both. To prove it, he welcomes Jester Fester, who tumbles on stage from the side door and lands a ta-da.

**The experiment:** count the balls Fester juggles and solve a math problem at the same time. Safadi invites the class, and the audience watching, to try along. The slide shows the two tasks.

**Trial 1:** Fester juggles five balls. *"Count the balls! And… what is 7 × 6?"* In row 3, Housam counts on his fingers while his eyes chase the balls. Alma's head bobs along until she starts sweating. *"Time!"* Housam guesses six, then four. Alma forgot the math. Safadi gives the answers: five balls, 42. *"Let's try a second time."*

**Trial 2:** Fester ups the stakes to seven balls. *"Count the balls!"* … *"What is 9 × 8?"* While Safadi is still asking, Fester's eyes drift up and a thought bubble pops up: *9 × 8…?* His hands stop and the cascade flies apart. BONK on Fester, BONK on Safadi, BONK on Fester again. The rest bounce across the stage. Both are dazed, with stars. Rubbing his head, Safadi smiles: *"Even the juggler can't multitask!"* Housam and Alma laugh. Fester gives a sheepish shrug, and the last ball, which went up out of frame, comes down and balances on his hat. Everybody grins. Iris out.

**Theme:** we do one thing at a time. Split your attention and both tasks suffer, even for a professional juggler.

## Notes for editing

- **Juggling:** both trials use `cascade()` (5 balls, then 7). `popIn()` flies each ball from the hat tip into its place in the pattern, so the cascade can start mid-pattern with no wind-up.
- **The fumble** (`TF = 65.2`): each ball's position at TF is taken from the 7-ball cascade and handed to `bounce()`, an analytic bounce on the stage floor (so any frame can be rendered alone). `HITS` lists which ball hits whose head, and when. Ball `SKY` goes up out of frame and comes back onto Fester's hat at `LAST_LAND`.
- **Slide:** `slide(t)` changes with the script: the spotlight idea, then the two tasks, the questions, and the answers.
- **Students:** the same row-3 seats and reverse-angle camera as scene 07. Safadi's off-screen bubble points off the bottom of the frame.
- **Safadi's stars** are drawn by the scene (`dazeStars`), since his rig has no `stars` option.
