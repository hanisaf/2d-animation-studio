## Synopsis

After school, Danny and Jenna stand in front of the sofa while Housam tells them what he learned today. A card pops up over his head for each class. Science was ecology, math was the Pythagorean theorem and social studies was the economy. In band he practiced the trumpet, and he plays a fanfare to prove it. Alma stands beside him and copies every sentence, every gesture and finally every toot on an air trumpet. Housam gets redder and redder until he explodes: *"ALMA! STOP REPEATING ME!!!"* Danny and Jenna crack up.

Professor Safadi comes in through the archway: *"Housam! Why are you yelling?"* He asks Alma to stop, and she repeats him too, right down to his thoughtful *"…Hmm."* Then his idea bulb pops on. *"Very well, Alma. Repeat after me."* He reels off one tongue twister after another, and Alma gabbles each one worse than the last while the room laughs. After the slippery snails her tongue is in a knot and stars circle her head. *To be continued…*

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`)

- **Timing comes from the dialogue.** The action times are read from tagged lines in `asset.js` (`toot`, `yell`, `idea`, `tw1`…`tw6`, `tied`), and each flub is the line after its twister. Retime a line in `asset.js` and its shots and laughs follow. The tongue-twister cuts are built by `twisterShots`.
- **Blocking** (`POS`, world units, sofa view): Danny (250, 205) and Jenna (−250, 205) in front of the sofa; Housam (95, 445) and Alma (−95, 455) in front of the coffee table. Safadi walks from the archway `SF_FROM` to `SF_TO` during `WALK`. Characters are depth-sorted and interleaved with `livingRoom.front`.
- **Subject cards:** `CARDS` gives each card's window; `ttCard` / `ttIcon` draw them. **Trumpet:** `ttTrumpet`, held in `HORN`, played in `PLAY`, toots at `TOOTS`.
- **Laughs:** `snicker` (part 1) and `laughing` (after the yell and each flub) drive Danny, Jenna, Housam and Safadi's laugh poses and the HA HA! / HEE HEE! effects.
- To add a twister, add a `twN` line for Safadi and Alma's flub after it in `asset.js`, then add the tag to `TW`.
