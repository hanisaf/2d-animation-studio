# Song prompt for Gemini: "The Trolls"

Please write and produce an original song for an animated music video: the finale of a four-episode kids' cartoon series called **The Trolls**. I will animate the video to your recording afterwards, so the song leads and the picture follows.

## The world of the show

A warm, goofy family cartoon about a professor, his twin brother, four kids and their friends. In the series, "trolling" means playful teasing between family and friends, never meanness. Everyone gets teased, everyone laughs, nobody gets hurt.

**The family and friends**
- **Professor Safadi:** a formal, proud professor in a tan suit and red tie. He loves to say "Allow me to explain…" and the kids love to stop him.
- **Doctor Sami:** his twin brother, a goofy, warm doctor in a white coat with a stethoscope.
- **Housam:** a sporty teenage boy, soccer and trumpet.
- **Alma:** his little sister, a cheeky copycat who loves to draw and dance.
- **Danny:** a funny six-year-old who loves building blocks and Rubik's cubes.
- **Jenna:** Alma's cousin, a gymnast who does cartwheels.
- **Sarah:** a rebellious ninth grader who loves texting, woodworking and volleyball. When she gets mad her hair spikes up.
- **Roudaynah:** a loving, busy mom and programmer who is never without her laptop.
- **Dr. G:** a gentle doctor-teacher who loves rules, healthy food and her clipboard.
- **Princess Pearl:** a plush rainbow dolphin princess who has never laughed at a joke.
- **King Neptune:** a plush blue dolphin with a trident who calls himself a king. He is not a king.
- **Dragon:** a friendly, curious dragon who can fly.

**The story so far**
1. **Tongue Twisters:** Alma copies everything her brother Housam says, until the professor stops her with tongue twisters she can't say. ("She sells sea-smells by the… she-sore?")
2. **Super Tongue Twisters:** the kids turn it into a game. Everyone challenges the next person, and every flub ends in a gag. "BROCCOLI SOCKS!" Danny quacks like a duck. Jenna squeaks like a dolphin. The professor gets "SUGARLESS SAFADI SIPPY-SIPS!" Doctor Sami tickles himself. Sarah's hair goes full rebellion. Roudaynah says hers perfectly ("…semicolon."), and Dr. G declares "RULE NUMBER RADISH!"
3. **Troll Analytics:** the professor's videos get about 21 views each, but the tongue-twister videos get millions. "People these days like silly things." "Let us double down on silliness!" The plan: wear costumes of the monsters Alma drew, go to school, and challenge the kids to tongue twisters. "That is the silliest thing I heard in ages!" "Exactly."

## This episode: the costumes go to school

The whole family marches to **Dove Creek Elementary**, a red-brick school with an arched steel canopy over the front doors, wearing costumes of **Alma's monster drawing** (attached). The costumes look hand-drawn, crayon-coloured and a bit lopsided, just like her picture:
- a big **grey bat** with blue-outlined wings, green spots and one fang-y grin;
- a round **brown fuzzy pumpkin monster** with three eyes, spiky teeth, furry legs and a blue wizard hat with black bats on it;
- a **white ghost** with a skull face, a stitched mouth and a little pumpkin candy bucket;
- a **pink ghost** with round ears and a sweet smile;
- a **striped owl** in a black top hat with blue zig-zag feathers and a grumpy frown;
- a **blue bird** with pink wings, a yellow beak and long yellow legs;
- a **yellow cat** with pink stripes and a surprised face;
- a **striped bee-butterfly** with curly antennae and golden wings.

(Rough casting, for your lyrics: the professor is the grumpy top-hat owl, Doctor Sami the big bat, Alma the pink ghost, Jenna the bee-butterfly, Danny the fuzzy pumpkin monster, Housam the blue bird, Sarah the yellow cat, Roudaynah the white ghost with the candy bucket and Dr. G the… whatever is left!)

**What happens, roughly in order** (an action-packed music video, like a heist movie crossed with a game show):
1. **The march:** at dawn the monster gang struts down the sidewalk to the school in slow motion, like heroes in a movie. Neighbours drop their coffee. The doors burst open.
2. **The hallway:** they sneak and tiptoe past lockers, then jump out at a corner. The school bell rings like a boxing bell.
3. **Classroom raids:** they storm one room after another, and in each one a monster challenges a student to a tongue twister. Students flub them gloriously, and then the students fire twisters back and the monsters flub too:
   - **the science room:** beakers bubble over and the twister explodes into foam;
   - **the library:** the librarian shushes, so they whisper-battle;
   - **the gym:** a dodgeball-speed twister showdown on the basketball court, with cartwheels and volleyball spikes;
   - **the math class:** a twister about numbers, and the professor nearly starts to explain before the kids shout "NO EXPLAINING!"
4. **The big battle:** the whole school piles into the gym: monsters on one side, students on the other, a twister tournament with a scoreboard. It gets faster and faster until nobody can talk and everyone collapses laughing.
5. **The reveal:** one by one the monsters pull off their costume heads (the professor, the doctor, the kids, the moms) and the school roars with laughter.
6. **The final twist:** the students turn to **Princess Pearl, King Neptune and Dragon**, who were at the back the whole time, and ask them to take off their costumes too. Pearl, Neptune and Dragon look at each other… "These aren't costumes!" And for the first time ever, **Princess Pearl laughs**. Everybody laughs. The end.

## The song

Please write an **original, silly, high-energy song** that is built almost entirely out of **creative, original tongue twisters**: monsters, school, broccoli, dolphins, rules, cubes, cartwheels, laptops, the professor's explaining. Let the singers really trip over the words. Flubs, giggles, tongue-tied gibberish, "say that five times fast!" and call-and-response challenges between the monsters and the students are all welcome.

- **Length:** about **3 minutes**, at a **steady tempo** (around 120 BPM, 4/4), so I can cut the animation on the beat.
- **Feel:** a big, bouncy, cartoon party anthem that keeps speeding up like a tongue twister, with a catchy chorus everyone can shout. Kid-friendly and clean, in clear English.
- **Voices:** two goofy grown-up men (the twins), bright kids, and a whole school of students as a choir.
- **End:** the costume reveal, then the dolphins' twist, and the song should **end in a big wave of laughter**.
