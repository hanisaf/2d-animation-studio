## Shot list

The shot list is built from the dialogue in `scene.js` (`SHOTS`). Every camera is framed automatically on whoever the shot is about (`frame`).

| Beat | Shot | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0 | `opening` | wide on the room, easing into the group |
| title | `titleShot` | group, with a shake on the title card |
| each `vsN` | `pair(from, to)` | the challenge; the VS banner slides up |
| the reply | `close(to)` | "Bring. It. ON." and so on |
| each `twN` | `close(from)` | the twister, conducted with a wagging finger |
| each `flN` | `flubShot` | a slow push in on the victim, a shake as the tongue knots |
| after each flub | `group()` | the laugh, the gag card, the dizzy stars; newcomers walk in here |
| `noexplain` | `noExplain` | the kids shout down the professor, with a shake |
| `ha8` | `ending` | the last big laugh, the freeze, the card, the iris |

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`)

- **Timing comes from the dialogue.** `ROUNDS` reads the tagged lines in `asset.js` (`vsN`, `twN`, `flN`, `haN`; round 7's flub is `perfect`). Each laugh runs from the end of the flub until somebody speaks again. Retime a line and its shots, poses, cards and laughs follow. The dialogue was laid out with a small generator: each beat starts a fixed gap after the previous line ends.
- **Blocking** (`SPOT`, world units, sofa view, +X is screen-left): the kids and the professor where episode one ended (Safadi has moved to the window side). Newcomers walk the polylines in `PATH` from the archway during `WALK` (from their first line).
- **Framing:** `frame(names, t, o)` centres the named characters and picks the focal length to fit them (`fill` for width, `hFill` for height). `groupCam` eases a newcomer into the group framing as they arrive. In close-ups and two-shots, anyone nearer the camera than the subjects is left out of the shot (`stage(…, focus)`).
- **Poses:** `pose()` handles the shared beats for every rig (talking, pointing, the flub, tongue-tied, laughing, stunned), using each rig's hand targets in `HANDS` and falling back on face names a rig lacks (`face`). `special()` holds the one-off gags.
- **Overlays:** `titleCard`, `vsBanner`, `gagCard` (art in `GAG`, keyed by round), the laugh SFX per character (`LAUGH_TXT`), QUACK! / EEE-EEE! / TICKLE TICKLE! / chirp.
- To add a round, add `vsN`, a reply, `twN`, `flN` and `haN` lines in `asset.js`, add the pair to `PAIRS` and a card to `GAG`.
