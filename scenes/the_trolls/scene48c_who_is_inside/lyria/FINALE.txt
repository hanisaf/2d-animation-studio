The encore of a silly, high-energy kids' cartoon game-show pop song, "Who's Inside?", the same band and the same singers as the song before it. In the key of C major, a strict steady 120 BPM in 4/4 the whole way through (one bar = 2 seconds), no tempo changes, so the video can be cut on the beat. Bright bouncy game-show pop: punchy drums, slap bass, brass stabs, glockenspiel, handclaps, a bubbly synth lead. Voices: two goofy grown-up men (twin brothers) singing lead, with bright kids and a whole audience of children as a choir; no adult female voice. Clean, clear English diction, kid-friendly, upbeat and fast like the rest of the song.
