# Scene 21c: The Book Fair

> The book fair opens in the DCES library. Two stands: the Safadi brothers selling ***Professor Safadi Explains…***, and the kids selling ***Dolphin Royalty*** coloring flip books. Students pour in and mob the kids' stand; only three wander over to Safadi's. Story time: Alma tells Fester's stories (*the juggling disaster* and *Fired!*), playing in a cloud over the story rug, while Safadi explains the Riemann hypothesis to three students who fall asleep. So does Sami. The kids sell every book; Safadi sells none, and is proud of them anyway: *"You read books… and now you write books!"* Only Alma is sad: *"I just wish Princess Pearl and Jester Fester were real…"* *"We can always imagine."* *To be continued…*

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`)

- **Students:** twelve, built from `studentLook(101 + 3i)`. Students 0–8 are the kids' crowd and 9–11 are Safadi's three. `stuAt(i, t)` gives each one's whole day as a path: in at the door, to `SPOT[i]` at a stand, to `SEAT[i]` for story time, to `SALE_SPOT[i]` for the sale, then out. `stuPose` does the acting: waving, sitting (`sit` ramps in and out), star eyes, laughing, dozing (`asleep()`), and the jolt at `WAKE`.
- **Story clouds:** `overWorld` draws `dryPop` (from scene 21b) at `POP` with `bfStory(kind, t − start)` inside it. That draws the throne room through its own `persp` camera, scaled into the cloud. `bfJuggle` and `bfFired` in `props.js` hold the beats of scenes 01 and 02, timed to Alma's lines.
- **The lesson:** `BOARDS` sets which chalkboard shows when. They are `bfBoard('primes' | 'count' | 'zeta' | 'strip')`, redrawn from scene 06's boards. A board is skipped when the camera is so close that it would fill the frame.
- **The sale:** the books fly from the stand into each buyer's hand at `BUY0 + i·BUY_DT`. `stock(t)` empties the table, and SOLD OUT! slaps onto the banner at `SOLD`.
- **The stands** (`bfStand`) are a cloth table with a banner on poles and books on top: the girls' real covers (`dryCover`) or Safadi's navy books (`bfSafadiBook`).
