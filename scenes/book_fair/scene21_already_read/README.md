Surprised, he switches to the power of writing, then brags about the books he wrote: *Professor Safadi Explains Mathematics… Physics… Astronomy…* The kids cut in: next week is the book fair. Will his books be popular? *"Of course. You shall see."* The kids whisper. *"…What are you planning?"* *"You shall see!"* *To be continued…*

## Synopsis

**The lecture.** A sunny morning on the DCES entrance plaza. Housam, Alma, Jenna and Danny stand in a row while Professor Safadi switches on his powers: *READING!* He counts the benefits on a floating chalkboard. **One:** reading wires your brain to be attentive and focused (just as a butterfly floats by and Danny's eyes follow it away). **Two:** it grows your vocabulary. **Three:** it builds your imagination. **Four:** it lets you walk in someone else's shoes. That's empathy. With each benefit the kids sag a little more: Jenna doodles, Housam juggles his ball on one knee, Alma sways in place, Danny yawns an enormous yawn. *"And FIVE! Reading improves your memo—"*

**The proof.** *"PROFESSOR!"* We know! We read *all the time*! *"Like, a LOT!"* The kids grab his sleeves and tow him through the glass doors.

**The library.** Each kid pulls a favourite from the shelves, and the story pops out around them: **Housam:** *City Spies* (spy shades drop onto his nose). **Alma:** *Warriors* (clan cats leap through a moonlit forest). **Jenna:** *Wings of Fire* (dragons swoop overhead, and the dragon in the mural winks). **Danny:** *Cat Kid Comic Club* (comic panels, POW!). All together, books high: **"We ALREADY read a lot!"**

**The pivot.** Safadi's powers fizzle out. *"…Oh."* The library clock ticks. He strokes his chin… and an idea bulb pops on. If they are such good readers, let's talk about **writing**! Writing is the foundation of civilization: marks pressed into clay, five thousand years ago, let ideas outlive the people who had them. And it's good for your brain: writing makes your **thinking clear**, helps you **remember**, and **organizes** your ideas into a beginning, a middle and an end.

**The brag.** Safadi straightens his tie. He knows a thing or two about writing. Cut to **his study, late at night**: fire crackling, clock ticking, Newton's cradle clicking, Safadi scribbling at his desk while the paper stack grows and crumpled drafts fly over his shoulder. He stamps **THE END**. Back in the library, he pulls three books out of his jacket, each with his own big face on the cover: ***Professor Safadi Explains Mathematics***, ***Professor Safadi Explains Physics***, ***Professor Safadi Explains Astro—***

**The cliffhanger.** The kids interrupt: next week is the **BOOK FAIR**. Do you think your books will be popular? *"Of course! I will be selling my books… myself."* A big smile: *"And you shall see that everyone will like my books."* The kids look at each other, smile, and huddle to whisper. Safadi's smile slips; a sweat drop. *"…What are you planning?"* Four sweet smiles: **"You shall see!"** **TO BE CONTINUED…**

**Theme:** reading and writing are two halves of the same thing, and the kids are better readers than the professor thought. (The *"you shall see"* is Safadi's own line, which the kids throw back at him, echoing scene 16.)

## Notes for editing (`scene.js`)

- `housamPose`, `almaPose`, `jennaPose`, `dannyPose` and `safadiPose` hold each character's acting over the whole scene, so a pose carries across cuts. Shots only choose the camera.
- Each set has its own staging constants: `DCES_SPOTS`, `LIB_SPOTS` and the study `MARK`. The library walk-in (53.6–55.8) goes from the door (−500, 0, 1195) down the centre aisle to `LIB_SPOTS`.
- `POWER` holds Safadi's power windows: on 7.4–43.2 (DCES), fizzle at 71.0, on 78.0–110.2 (library), and a small glow on each book at 124.2–132.6. `BOARD` is the board's world point in each set, so every shot agrees where it floats.
- `POPS` holds each story pop: kind, owner, start and the world point it's pinned to (for Jenna's, the bunting and the mural dragon).
- Housam carries his soccer ball at DCES and drops it by the door in the library. Jenna holds her sketchbook throughout, except while she holds up *Wings of Fire* (62.2–71.0).
- The study is the night version; its laptop and chalkboard aren't featured.
