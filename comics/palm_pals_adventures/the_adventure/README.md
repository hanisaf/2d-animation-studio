# The Adventure — *Palm Pals Adventures #1*

> **Written & originally illustrated by Jenna Safadi** (`Original Drawing 1.jpg`, `Original Drawing 2.jpg`).  
> Adapted into an 8-page Letter-format comic book using the studio's `palm_pals` rig and `engine/comic.js`, configured for per-panel Gemini Nano Banana 2.1 refinement (`mode: 'panel'`) with crisp vector lettering.

## Story Overview

Ten-year-old **Eve Earth** wakes up to the smell of Dad's sizzling pancakes — until the doorbell rings and in walks his worst "enemy": six-year-old **Zelie Pink Lemonade** (*"Just call me Zelie"*) and her Papa! After a loud rivalry showdown and a lightning-speed pancake breakfast, Eve sneaks to his room to test a mysterious portal button. Zelie barges in, pushes past him, and leaps straight into the swirling blue portal! Forced to rescue her, Eve jumps in too — popping out in an outdoor adventure land wearing a brown plaid explorer hat while the portal locks shut behind a sign: *"Find the key to open the portal."* Ten chaotic hours (and one bear encounter!) later, they burst out laughing, make up, and discover the key was hiding under Eve's itchy hat the whole time. Back home they immediately get put in **Time Out** by both Dads — only to become best friends anyway!

## Cast

- **Eve Earth** (`palm_pals` · `'eve'`): 10 years old. Sky-blue plushie globe with stitched green continents, stubby blue plushie arms & feet for holding props, and a brown plaid explorer hat in the portal world.
- **Zelie Pink Lemonade** (`palm_pals` · `'zelie'`): 6 years old (*"Just call me Zelie"*). Pink lemonade plushie cup with white whipped foam rim, yellow lemon slice, striped pink straw, and stubby pink plushie arms & feet.
- **Eve's Dad** (custom larger, deeper-ocean Earth globe plushie with a squiggly mustache in the Palm Pals style): Cheerful pancake-flipping dad who turns stern when kids open secret bedroom portals.
- **Zelie's Papa** (custom larger, crimson-coral Fruit Punch / Strawberry Lemonade cup plushie with yellow straw, citrus wedge, and a squiggly mustache): Friendly visiting dad who teams up with Eve's Dad for Time Out duty.
