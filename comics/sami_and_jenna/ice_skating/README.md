# Ice Skating Adventure

> **Book**: [Sami & Jenna Adventures](../book.js) · **Cast**: [Dr. Sami](../../../characters/sami/README.md), [Jenna](../../../characters/jenna/README.md) (featuring Fafy the giraffe)

A two-page family comic based on Jenna's original hand-drawn comic strip (`Original Drawing.jpg`) and Page 2 sketch (`Original Drawing 2.jpg`). Dr. Sami invites Jenna on an outing, Jenna grabs her winter jacket and plush giraffe Fafy, they wave goodbye to their pet goldfish, hit the Minnesota ice rink where Jenna shows off her skating tricks and encourages Dad, share a warm hug on the ice, and return home to relax together by the glowing fireplace.
