# Jester Fester: series bible

> The story of a court jester whose tricks always go *almost* right.

## Premise

Jester Fester is the royal court jester: cheerful, eager to please, and endlessly unlucky. Every episode he tries to make the court laugh, every trick goes almost right, and the universe (usually in the form of a juggling ball) finds his head. He always bounces back with a grin, because, as he puts it, *"this is the story of my life."*

## Tone and rules

- **Something happens in every shot.** A character acts, and something falls, flies, pops or breaks.
- **Tell jokes with acting.** Speech goes in short callout bubbles, one line at a time. Sound effects (POP!, BONK!, DING!) are used sparingly, on the hit.
- **Fester never loses his grin for long.** Setbacks end in a sheepish shrug at the camera, not in anger.
- **Recurring gags:** the ball that always finds his head; the chicken joke; *"…story of my life."*

## Look

- A clean 2D cartoon: flat, saturated character colours with plum ink outlines and a light hand-drawn "boil"; pastel sets with soft gradients; paper grain.
- Sets are real perspective spaces the camera moves through. Backgrounds soften when the camera is close, so the characters read.
- Every mood change is animated (a blink or squeeze, then the new face, then an emote); nothing snaps.
